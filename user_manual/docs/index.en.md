# Blink Call User Manual

**Blink Call** is an assistive call system designed for ALS patients. After the patient completes a preset sequence of eye-open and eye-closed actions, the computer or a Bluetooth speaker plays an alert to notify family members or caregivers.

## Who This Is For

- People who have difficulty speaking and cannot easily call out for help.
- People who have difficulty using their hands and cannot reliably press buttons or use a physical call device.
- People who can complete simple eye-open and eye-closed actions.
- Patients whose family members or caregivers can assist with initial setup and testing.

## Before You Start

- A Windows computer.
- A camera that can clearly capture both of the patient's eyes.
- Computer speakers or a Bluetooth speaker.
- An internet connection for the first model download.

## Before Daily Use Begins

Have the patient and caregiver complete at least one test call together and confirm that:

- The camera view is continuous and clear, with both eyes fully visible.
- The patient can complete the configured blink sequence.
- The computer or Bluetooth speaker alert can be heard clearly.
- The caregiver knows how to stop the alert.

Repeat the test after changes to the software or environment, such as replacing the camera, moving the computer, or adjusting the blink pattern.
