# Quick Start

This section helps family members or caregivers complete the initial setup and learn how to make daily calls.

## First-Time Setup

1. **Download and extract the software** to a fixed location.
2. **Open the software** by double-clicking `BlinkCall.exe`.
3. **Download the model files** under **Settings → Blink Call → Download/Update model files**.
4. **Check the camera view**: allow camera access and make sure both of the patient's eyes are fully visible.
5. **Complete a test call**: follow the top progress bar through the default blink sequence and confirm that the alert can be heard.

See [First Use](first-use.md) for the video, detailed steps, screenshots, and signs of successful setup.

## Daily Use

1. Open the software.
2. Check that the camera view is continuous and clear.
3. Make sure neither eye is covered.
4. Complete the configured blink sequence.
5. To stop the ringtone early, have a caregiver click the red call alert button.

See [Daily Use](daily-use.md) for details.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- The software will not open or model files will not download.
- There is no camera image, or no face is detected.
- Blink progress does not move, calls do not trigger, or calls trigger accidentally.
- A call is triggered but no alert can be heard.

For these problems, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Signs That Setup Is Complete

After the initial setup, all of the following should be true:

- The main screen continuously shows the patient's camera view.
- The top progress bar responds to eye-open and eye-closed actions.
- The patient can complete the sequence without excessive fatigue.
- The caregiver can hear the alert after the sequence finishes.
- The caregiver can stop the alert and knows where to adjust the blink pattern, camera, and audio.
